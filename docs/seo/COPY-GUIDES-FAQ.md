*Captures: made to measure vs bespoke, tailor made, custom made, have a dress made. Slug: `/guides/made-to-measure-vs-bespoke`*

- **Title:** Made-to-measure vs tailor-made vs bespoke | LINSILK
- **Meta:** The difference between made-to-measure, tailor-made, custom-made and bespoke clothing, and where LINSILK sits. Plain answers, no jargon.
- **H1:** Made-to-measure, tailor-made or bespoke: what is the difference?
- **Answer-first:** *Made-to-measure* and *tailor-made* both mean a garment cut and sewn to your own measurements; *bespoke* in strict tailoring adds a pattern drafted from scratch, fittings and a trial garment. LINSILK is made-to-measure (tailor-made): your measurements become a pattern, the piece is cut and sewn for you in 2–4 weeks, and there are no fittings.
- **H2 The five terms in one table:**
  | Term | What it usually means | LINSILK |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Off the rack | A standard size, made in batches | No |
  | Made to measure / tailor-made | Cut and sewn to your own measurements | **Yes** |
  | Custom-made | Made to your specification; often includes colour, length or design choices | Yes, through colour choice and the Design Your Own commission |
  | Made to order | Made after you order, not kept in stock | Yes: nothing is stocked |
  | Bespoke | A pattern drafted from scratch for you, with fittings and a toile | **No** |
- **H2 Why LINSILK does not do fittings:** Fittings need you and a tailor in the same room. LINSILK works at a distance: you measure yourself with a guide, or take it to any tailor, and the piece is inspected twice against your numbers before it is dispatched from Geneva. That is why a piece takes 2–4 weeks, not the two months a fitted couture dress usually takes.
- **H2 Which should you choose?** Choose made-to-measure if you know your measurements, want a dress or shirt in a specific cloth, and do not need to try it on mid-way. Choose bespoke if you want a one-off pattern and several fittings.
- **H2 Have a dress made online:** (link to `/made-to-measure`).
- **FAQ:** Is made-to-measure better than off the rack? · Is made-to-measure the same as tailor-made? · Is custom-made the same as made-to-measure? · What does bespoke mean?

*Captures: silk vs satin, Seide oder Satin, différence soie satin. Slug: `/guides/silk-or-satin`.*
